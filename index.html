QUESTIONS? FAIR.

Good to know.

Here's what most location owners ask before getting started.

Does it cost our location anything?+

Our standard pilot offer includes the machine, routine installation and servicing at no charge to approved host locations. We discuss access to power, placement and any special site work before agreeing.

Who handles stocking and maintenance?+

We do. SmartStuff manages product selection, restocking and routine machine maintenance so your staff doesn't have another job to do.

Can we request certain snacks or drinks?+

Absolutely. We want the assortment to fit your crowd, and we'll use requests and sales feedback to improve it. Product availability varies.

What if the pilot isn't a good fit?+

We'll review the results together at the end of the agreed pilot. The agreement will set out removal and other practical details upfront.

What areas do you serve?+

We're starting locally in Southeast Michigan, including the Novi, Wixom and South Lyon area. Reach out if you're nearby—we'll confirm whether we can serve your location.
