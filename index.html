GOOD QUESTIONS

Wondering
how it works?

Here's what we'd want to know, too.

What does the machine cost our facility?+

For approved host locations, our standard pilot offer includes the equipment and routine maintenance at no charge. Site suitability, power and any special installation requirements are discussed upfront.

Who refills and maintains the machine?+

We manage stock and routine service. You provide agreed access to the machine and power.

Can we request products?+

Absolutely. We'll discuss your audience before the pilot and adapt the assortment based on requests, availability and sales.

Is it only healthy snacks?+

No. We want to make better choices convenient without removing familiar favorites or treats. We don't claim every product is healthy.

Where are you operating?+

We're beginning in Southeast Michigan, focusing on the South Lyon, Novi and Wixom area and neighboring communities.
